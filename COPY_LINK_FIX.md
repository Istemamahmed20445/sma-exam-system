# ✅ Copy Link Fix Deployed!

## 🎯 What Was Fixed

The Copy Link button now requires students to enter their name and email before accessing the exam.

---

## 🔧 Changes Made

### **Before:**
- Copied direct exam URL: `http://yoursite.com/exam/ABC123`
- Students could access exam without entering name/email

### **After:**
- Copies shareable link: `http://yoursite.com/?exam=ABC123`
- Shows login form first
- Students must enter name and email
- Then proceeds to exam

---

## 📍 How It Works Now

1. **Click "Copy Link"** on any exam card
2. Link copied: `http://yoursite.com/?exam=ABC123`
3. **Share the link** with students
4. **When opened:**
   - Login form appears automatically
   - Student enters name and email
   - Click "Start Exam"
   - Proceeds to exam
